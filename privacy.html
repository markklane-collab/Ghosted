Privacy

Ghosted by the Machine: The Game · Last updated November 4, 2026

Short version: the game doesn't ask who you are to play. If you choose to join the email list, we use your email only to send Ghosted by the Machine updates. We never sell it.

Playing the game

You can play without an account, name, or email. Your progress stays on your device. We don't use cookies or advertising trackers.

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count things like visits, levels completed, and button clicks. It doesn't identify you and doesn't track you across other sites.

The email list (optional)

If you tap "Join the list," a Google Form opens where you can share your name, email, and whether you're a job seeker, recruiter, or hiring manager. Google stores those answers for us.

  • We use them only to send new levels, podcast episode drops, and job search tips from Mark at Ghosted by the Machine.
  • We never sell or rent your email, and we don't give the list to sponsors.
  • If a sponsor ever offers something, like a free résumé review, we'll only share your details if you separately ask for that offer.
  • Every email includes a way to unsubscribe. You can also ask us to delete your details at any time.

Feedback

The in-game star ratings and tags are anonymous. The written feedback form (a Google Form) may ask for your name and email, but those questions are optional. If you share them, we use them only to follow up on your feedback or, if you ask, to send updates. We never sell them or give them to sponsors.

Questions

Email mark@ghostedbythemachine.com to ask a question or have your details removed.
