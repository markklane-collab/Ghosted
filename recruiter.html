            Riley's takeaways
            • Different isn't fake. Accents, gaps, captions and bad internet are people, not red flags.
            • Look for mismatches, not quirks. Copied résumés, shared phone numbers, a new shipping address, and skipping paperwork are real warning signs.
            • Verify with a process, not a hunch. An ID check and a live skills exercise protect you and treat everyone the same.

            Riley's takeaways
            • Match the effort to the stage. A clear template is fine after an application. After an interview, people have earned a few personal lines.
            • Automate the early stage, not the hard conversations. Use bulk tools to buy time for the people who've met the team.
            • Closing without a message is still ghosting. If a tool offers to "clean up" your pipeline, check whether anyone hears back.
